# Deep-reading DOCX blueprint

Create a polished Chinese DOCX with restrained navy and blue accents, strong
black hierarchy, generous margins, readable body text, and clear tables. Let
evidence determine length. Keep the writing as direct and readable as the
first-pass essence.

## Front matter

1. **Cover** — `书名 深度书解与个人思考`.
2. **书目与版本说明** — author, translator when relevant, edition, source,
   language, pagination reliability, reading coverage, and analysis date.
3. **目录** — include every chapter and every second-level item, including
   question titles and table-based items that may not use heading styles.

## Required chapter order

1. **第一轮精华沉淀** — preserve the clearest version of the initial essence.
   Include `一句话主旨` and `全书主线`; explain the central question, causal path,
   decisive events, and main blind spot in plain language.
2. **作者的核心打法** — 4–7 repeatable practices. For each, state what the
   author did, why it worked, what it cost, and where it stops working.
3. **值得拿走的深度思考** — 4–7 durable judgments about risk, values, trust,
   power, responsibility, judgment, or human nature. Lead with the conclusion
   and use concrete evidence.
4. **书里容易被忽略的信息差** — surface mechanisms, incentives, timing,
   organizational facts, market structure, and narrative functions that casual
   reading misses.
5. **他最容易被学错的地方** — identify behaviors readers may imitate without
   the author's prerequisites. Explain the missing ability, condition, safety
   margin, or institutional support.
6. **如何客观看待这本书与作者的叙述** — explain why critical distance is
   needed, why the book should not be dismissed wholesale, and which claims or
   events require independent verification. Separate action, motive, outcome,
   and self-interpretation.
7. **跳出作者叙述看他的成功与失败** — attribute outcomes across ability,
   timing, systems, incentives, luck, external conditions, and accumulated
   costs. Include later developments only when they are independently sourced
   and clearly dated.
8. **我们这次聊清楚了什么** — reconstruct each core question with the
   reader's doubt, the reader's answer, the assistant's answer, the resulting
   judgment, and the unresolved point. Add a tentative portrait of the reader's
   decision style, its advantages, and its likely costs when the dialogue
   supports it.
9. **这本书对你有什么用** — organize by real work or life situations. Under
   every situation, write the author's specific action, the causal explanation,
   the lesson that transfers, and how the reader can use it. Do not leave an
   abstract recommendation without the author's concrete example.
10. **现在可以做的几件小事** — 3–6 small actions with observable signals.
    Include deliberate non-action when restraint is the better experiment.
11. **跨书连接** — show only meaningful resonance, contradiction, extension,
    or boundary retrieved from the private library. State the mechanism that
    changes understanding. Record the first-book state honestly.
12. **记忆检索卡：核心打法和使用例子** — use a compact table. Each row must
    contain one core tactic or thinking frame, one memorable sentence, and one
    vivid work or life example showing how to use it.
13. **作者的关键言论与观点** — collect short, high-value original statements
    or faithful original viewpoints. Mark exact wording versus paraphrase and
    cite the source location. Prefer a few useful lines over decorative quotes.
14. **来源与解释说明** — list the book source, external verification sources,
    coverage limits, dates, and the boundary between author statements,
    independent facts, analysis, and reader response.

## Writing rules

- Use the first-pass essence as the content spine. Preserve its direct language
  and reading order whenever accurate.
- Lead with conclusions, mechanisms, choices, costs, and consequences. Replace
  theory-heavy labels with observable actions and plain Chinese.
- Give the reader-response chapter enough space to preserve the real reasoning
  arc. Keep the reader's language distinct from the assistant's interpretation.
- State uncertainty precisely. Do not invent motives, memories, opinions,
  quotes, evidence, or personal traits.
- Follow the banned-construction rule in `SKILL.md` across the DOCX, dialogue,
  card, and questions.
- Use tables only for real comparisons, memory retrieval, or compact structured
  information. Keep visible light-gray borders, deliberate widths, comfortable
  padding, and repeating header rows for multi-page tables.
- Keep quotations short and necessary. Paraphrase surrounding material and cite
  precise pages or sections.

## Layout and verification rules

- Use readable Chinese fonts with reliable fallback and verify all glyphs in a
  rendered copy.
- Use black headings. Reserve blue for the title, small accents, numbering, and
  table headers.
- Fit a long detailed TOC efficiently. Use a balanced two-column layout when a
  single column would create sparse continuation pages or large blank areas.
- Finalize body pagination before writing TOC numbers. Use verified static page
  numbers in the delivery copy; never ship placeholder or stale `PAGEREF`
  results. Re-render and check every TOC destination.
- Inspect every page at 100%. Reject clipping, overlap, broken tables, missing
  glyphs, orphaned headings, false blank pages, or large avoidable white areas.
- Never include internal notes, temporary paths, prompts, machine metadata, or
  private library content in the final DOCX.
